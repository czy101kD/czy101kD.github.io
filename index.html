True stories from the past, retold as small animated tales.

Every story here really happened. A war fought against birds. A fortune lost on a single trade. An invention nobody wanted until everybody did. We pick a moment from history, check the facts, and tell it the way a good storyteller would: short, vivid, and true.

New tales arrive as short animated reels on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

About ReelBot

Once Upon A Fact uses a small private tool, ReelBot, to help turn our researched scripts into animated reels and to upload the reels we have reviewed and approved to our own channels. ReelBot is used only by the channel's creator and only for the channel's own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.
